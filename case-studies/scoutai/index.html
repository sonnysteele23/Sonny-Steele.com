The challenge

We hypothesized that building a single platform combining verified job matching, AI-tailored application materials, and ghost job filtering — three needs no competitor addressed — would collapse a 6-tool workflow into one and dramatically reduce wasted effort.

Competitive research

Landscape Audit

Before designing anything, I conducted a systematic competitive analysis of 40+ products across 8 market categories. Every competitor was evaluated on features, pricing, UX quality, and user reviews.

The key finding: No single platform combined job matching, resume tailoring, cover letter generation, ghost filtering, AND application tracking. Users needed 3–4 separate tools.

Three features had zero coverage across all 40+ competitors: ghost job detection, AI humanization controls, and integrated BLS salary data. These became ScoutAI's core differentiators.

User Research

Key findings that shaped the design.

Ghost Job Epidemic
30–40% of listings are expired, filled, or fake — wasting hours of effort per week with no feedback or signal
AI Detection Anxiety
Job seekers reported hiring managers flagging AI-generated cover letters — but had no way to control the tone
Fragmented Workflow
Seekers juggle 6+ disconnected tools — LinkedIn, job boards, Google Docs, spreadsheets, AI writers, email
Salary Blindness
43% of postings hide salary — candidates waste time on roles below their range with no integrated market data
Sonny Steele|sonny@sonny-steele.com
Design Decisions

Three tradeoffs shaped every design decision:

Automation vs. user agency — Auto-apply competitors like LazyApply (1.9★) spam hundreds of generic applications. Users lose control. Solution: a token-based system where users review and approve each application, with a humanization slider that gives explicit control over AI-generated tone.
Information density vs. clarity — Job seekers need salary, match score, skills, location, and posting age visible at a glance — but overload causes decision paralysis. Solution: progressive disclosure with a compact card showing key signals, expanding to full detail on interaction.
Single dashboard vs. specialized views — Competitors force users between separate apps for search, resume building, and tracking. Solution: one dashboard where filtering, evaluating, generating materials, and applying all happen without navigation — reducing the 6-tool workflow to one screen.
